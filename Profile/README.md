## 👋 Who We Are

Most businesses don't struggle because they lack effort. They struggle because their day-to-day work is tangled up in spreadsheets, repeated manual tasks, disconnected tools, and processes nobody has had time to fix.

**Artowix** exists to untangle that. We look at the real problems our clients face and build the right solution for them, whether that's a custom application, an AI-powered tool, an automated workflow, or a properly configured network and IT setup. The goal is always the same: make the work **straightforward and easy**.


## 🛠️ What We Do

### 💻 Custom Applications & ERP
Off-the-shelf tools don't always fit the way your business actually works. We design and build applications and ERP systems around your processes, such as web apps, internal tools, dashboards, and integrations, so your team works with the system instead of around it.

### 🤖 AI Solutions & Automation
We apply AI and automation where they make a real difference: answering repetitive questions, processing documents, organizing data, and supporting better decisions. If someone on your team does the same task over and over, it can probably be automated. We connect your tools, remove manual handoffs, and build workflows that run reliably in the background, saving time and reducing errors.

### 🌐 Network & IT Configuration
Good software needs a solid foundation. We set up and configure business networks and IT infrastructure so everything stays connected, secure, and running smoothly.


## 🎯 How We Approach Every Project

| Step | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| **1. Understand** | We learn how your business works and where the friction is. |
| **2. Simplify** | We map the workflow and cut out what doesn't need to exist. |
| **3. Build** | We deliver the right fit: applications, AI and automation, or IT setup, or a combination. |
| **4. Support** | We make sure it works in practice and keep improving it with you. |

The right solution depends on the problem, so we don't push a one-size-fits-all package. We start with what's actually slowing you down.


## 🤝 Who We Work With

Businesses that want to spend less time on busywork and more time on growth, from teams still running on spreadsheets and email to companies ready to bring AI and automation into their operations.
